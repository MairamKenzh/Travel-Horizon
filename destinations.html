Issyk-Kul Lake

The "pearl of Kyrgyzstan": about 180 km long, at 1,600 m altitude. Swim in summer, visit Cholpon-Ata's petroglyphs, and enjoy mountain views from every beach.

📍 Issyk-Kul☀️ Jun–Sep🚗 4–5 h from Bishkek
Lake · NomadsView details

Song-Kul Lake

A remote alpine lake at around 3,000 m surrounded by summer pastures. Stay in yurt camps run by herder families and go horse riding.

📍 Naryn☀️ Jun–Sep🐎 Horse treks
Alpine lakeView details

Ala-Kul Lake

A turquoise glacial lake at 3,532 m in the Teskey Ala-Too range. It can only be reached on foot, on a classic 2–3 day trek from Karakol.

📍 Issyk-Kul☀️ Jul–Sep🥾 Trek only
Hidden gemView details

Kel-Suu Lake

An emerald lake squeezed between sheer rock walls near the Chinese border. About four hours by car from Naryn, then on foot or on horseback.

📍 Naryn☀️ Jun–Sep🛂 Border permit
National parkView details

Ala-Archa National Park

A gorge 40 km south of Bishkek with trails to waterfalls and the Ak-Sai glacier. Easy access for a day hike.

📍 Chuy🗓️ All year🚕 1 h from Bishkek
Adventure hubView details

Karakol

Base for treks to Ala-Kul lake and Altyn-Arashan hot springs. See the wooden Holy Trinity Cathedral and the Dungan Mosque, and ski in winter.

📍 Issyk-Kul🗓️ All year
Hot springsView details

Altyn-Arashan

A green alpine valley above Karakol with a rushing river, spruce forests and natural hot springs. Soak in the warm pools after a day of hiking.

📍 Issyk-Kul☀️ May–Oct♨️ Hot springs
Red rocksView details

Jeti-Oguz

"Seven Bulls": striking red sandstone cliffs and the Broken Heart rock, with a lush valley perfect for hiking and picnics.

📍 Issyk-Kul☀️ May–Oct
CanyonView details

Skazka (Fairy Tale) Canyon

Colourful wind-carved rock formations on the southern shore of Issyk-Kul that look like castles and dragons.

📍 Issyk-Kul☀️ Apr–Oct
WaterfallsView details

Barskoon Gorge

A forested gorge on the south shore of Issyk-Kul with tall waterfalls and a monument to Yuri Gagarin, the first person in space, who rested here.

📍 Issyk-Kul☀️ May–Oct💧 Waterfalls
Silk RoadView details

Burana Tower

An 11th-century minaret from the ancient city of Balasagun, surrounded by stone balbal grave markers. Easy half-day trip from Bishkek.

📍 Near Tokmok🗓️ All year
Silk RoadView details

Tash-Rabat

A 15th-century stone caravanserai hidden in a valley at 3,500 m, on the old route towards China. Stay overnight in nearby yurts.

📍 Naryn☀️ Jun–Sep
UNESCOView details

Osh & Sulaiman-Too

One of the oldest cities in Central Asia. Climb the sacred Sulaiman-Too mountain and explore the huge Jayma Bazaar.

📍 Osh🗓️ All year✈️ Flights from Bishkek

Bishkek

📍 Chuy🗓️ All year
ForestView details

Arslanbob

Home to one of the largest natural walnut forests in the world, with waterfalls and a welcoming village homestay scene.

Sary-Chelek Lake

A deep-blue lake within a UNESCO biosphere reserve, surrounded by spruce and fruit forests. Quiet and off the beaten path.
